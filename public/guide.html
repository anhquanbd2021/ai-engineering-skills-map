Guide: how the meter maps to the study

Each widget in the lab answers one question the article raises.

The four axes

Skill clusterArticle sectionWhat coverage measures
Building & deploying AI applications§1 The building blocks (RAG, context engineering, agentic workflows) plus the evals/error-analysis loops the letter calls the core skill.
Software engineering fundamentals§2 Trade-off literacy — the layer that lets you steer the trade-offs a coding agent makes for you.
Using coding agents§3 Calibrated autonomy: context management, plan-vs-execute, verifiers that let agents check their own work.
Shaping the build§4 Spec-side judgment: product sense, business context, MVP-vs-careful tempo.

The two bars per skill

Study emphasis (hatched bar): derived mechanically from how many sub-skills the published letter names for that cluster, normalized to the richest list. It is an editorial estimate of prominence inside the study — the letter does not publish per-skill posting frequencies.

Your coverage (solid bar): the mean of your 0–3 self-ratings across that skill's named sub-skills. Reflective, not a measurement.

The verdict

Breadth = axes at ≥50% coverage (literate). Depth = strongest axis at ≥66%. A T-shaped profile needs both: depth on one axis, literacy on at least three. Two flags mirror the study's warnings:

FlagTriggerWhy the study cares
leverage-without-steeringagents ≥66% and fundamentals <50% You can generate fast but can't audit the trade-offs being made for you (§2).
builder-without-compassAI-app build ≥66% and shaping <50% Fast at building, unsteered on what to build (§4).

Honest limits

The letter itself is Part 1 of an evolving map; Ng says it will be updated. Emphasis scores are mechanical derivations, not requisition counts. A 0–3 self-rating measures reflection, not ability — use it to pick a learning order, not to grade yourself.
